Stories for Teenagers: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Stories for Teenagers ("the app") is published on Google Play by FindLogics. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no internet permission.

Information kept on your phone

To work, the app saves a few settings on your own phone only: your language, text size, reading colours, saved and finished stories, where you stopped reading, your reading streak, and your reminder time. This never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

Read aloud

The Listen button uses the text-to-speech voice already installed on your phone. The story text is processed by that voice engine on your device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows one daily reminder with the story of the day at the time you choose. You can turn it off in the app's Settings or in your phone's settings.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
