Devotional Songs: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Devotional Songs ("the app") is published on Google Play by FindLogics. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Information the app collects

The app itself does not collect, store on any server, or sell any personal information. It has no sign-in, no ads of its own and no analytics.

Songs and YouTube API Services

Songs play inside the YouTube player, from the music labels' own YouTube channels. The app uses YouTube API Services. When a song plays, YouTube (Google) receives information such as your device's IP address, device and browser details, cookies or similar identifiers, and which video is played, and may show its own ads. Google handles this information under the Google Privacy Policy. By playing songs in the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

The app does not download or keep any video or audio, and it does not access your YouTube or Google account.

Information kept on your phone

The app saves a few settings on your own phone only: your language, text size, reading colours, saved songs and prayers, and your reminder time. This never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

Internet and network state

Internet is used only to play songs. The app checks whether you are online so it can show a picture and the prayer text instead of a song when you are offline.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows one morning reminder with the day's deity and song at the time you choose. You can turn it off in the app's Settings or in your phone's settings.

Prayer texts

The stotrams and keerthanas are traditional texts in the public domain. Their meanings are simple explanations written for this app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
